# KETUK. — Complete Vertical Slice Chapters 1–3

## Purpose

This branch is the first **single playable arc** instead of a sequence of isolated prototypes.

The player can start the project with F5 and play through:

```
CHAPTER 1 — BARANG YANG TIDAK DILIHAT ORANG
        ↓
CHAPTER 2 — BATAS
        ↓
CHAPTER 3 — ORANG YANG TEPAT
        ↓
SENTANA PRIVATE AUCTION
INVITE REQUIRED
        ↓
END OF CURRENT VERTICAL SLICE
```

No P0.x screen is required to complete the story flow.

## Chapter 1

### Opening
- cash: Rp430.000
- kiosk arrears: Rp1.200.000
- deadline: Minggu 20:00
- old family book
- margin note:
  > Kalau ragu, lihat bagian yang tidak dilihat orang.

### Auction
Three lots:
1. rice cooker
2. mixed box
3. analog camera

Core:
- investigate before bidding
- read NPC behavior
- BID / WAIT / STOP
- post-lot social reaction
- ownership follows actual results

### Camera
Pak Harun appraisal is not automatic.

Knowledge is only carried forward if the player actually witnesses the inspection.

### Home
If Lot 02 was won:
- open Mixed Box
- inspect Coaster / Table Lighter / Adapter
- write temporary observations
- Book entry follows actual paid price

If Lot 02 was lost:
- no box appears at home
- no fake ownership entry

## Chapter 2 — BATAS

Core question:
> How much value is useful if it cannot become money before the deadline?

Starting point:
- Sunday 16:00
- kiosk deadline Sunday 20:00
- arrears Rp1.200.000

Routes:

### A — No Camera
No fake camera is created.
Deadline passes.
Kiosk is lost.

### B1 — Sell Now
- camera shop: Rp1.650.000
- immediate liquidity
- kiosk survives

### B2 — Adi
- Rp2.100.000 for lens only
- body returned
- Pak Arman extends deadline to Monday 10:00
- transaction Monday 09:00
- kiosk survives

### B3-A — Stop Chasing
- chase Sentana until 18:00
- stop
- late camera-shop offer: Rp1.500.000
- kiosk survives

### B3-B — Chase Too Long
- continue searching
- deadline passes
- kiosk lost
- Monday morning Sentana is proven real
- offer: Rp3.800.000

The highest number does not erase time.

## Chapter 3 — ORANG YANG TEPAT

Core principle:
> Buku memberi fragmen, manusia memberi konteks.

### Map
Pasar Tua is navigated by place, not by universal expert menu:
- Pintu Pasar
- Kios Tengah
- Kedai Pojok
- Gang Timur
- Lorong Belakang
- Papan Pengumuman

### People
Natural knowledge chain:
1. Pak Wira
2. Pak Damar
3. Bu Sari
4. Yanto

The player may explore out of order, but each person has a limited domain.

### If Lot 02 is owned
- the physical coaster can be discussed
- Bu Sari can establish that the yellow surface is a layer
- it is not solid brass
- Yanto can inspect the old lighter mechanism without claiming identity/rarity

### If Lot 02 is not owned
Nobody pretends the object is present.
The chapter becomes network-building rather than fake appraisal.

### Book
The Book starts recording useful people, not diary prose:
- Pak Wira — network
- Pak Damar — who knows whom
- Bu Sari — metal/material
- Yanto — small mechanisms

### Cliffhanger
After the network is established, the player travels back toward the notice board.

Poster:
- SENTANA PRIVATE AUCTION
- BY INVITATION ONLY

The MC **photographs** the poster.
The MC does not tear it down.

## Continuity Rules Locked by This Slice

1. Lost items never appear in MC inventory/home.
2. Information not witnessed is not magically known.
3. Specialists have boundaries.
4. Referral gives a lead, not teleportation.
5. Map displays places before people when appropriate.
6. Price is not the same as liquidity.
7. Wealth outcome is not treated as moral score.
8. Failure changes the story; it is not automatically game over.
9. Book notes are practical records, not omniscient narration.
10. Sentana remains uncertain until evidence proves otherwise.
